What this schedule models

Endurance is a time budget, not a measured fuel or battery curve. Aircraft recover with the specified reserve, then receive a full budget after turnaround. Transit is a straight illustrative connection to and from the route.

One sensor pointing direction is used for all cells at each sample. A pattern may have aircraft on station while its footprint leaves the task partly or completely unobserved. Finite searches execute one pass per sortie.

Results use deterministic midpoint samples and equal-weight grid cells. Reported gap edges are whole-bin estimates. No wind, terrain, weather, real threat system, flight-control interface or validated platform performance is included. The comparison returns the best option tested within a bounded set, not a guaranteed global optimum.
